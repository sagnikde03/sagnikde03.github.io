Hi! I completed my B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering in 2025 from the Institute of Radio Physics and Electronics (IRPE), [University of Calcutta](https://www.caluniv.ac.in/), India. I also completed a Minor in Business Administration from [Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Patna](https://www.iitp.ac.in).

My research interests include neuroengineering, ubiquitous computing, medical imaging, brain-computer interfaces, LLMs in healthcare and wearable technologies. More recently, I have been working on Generative AI, particularly Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Model Context Protocol (MCP), and agentic AI systems for information retrieval, tool use, and intelligent automation, with a focus on developing applied AI solutions for real-world enterprise clients.

Previously, I was a Winter Research Intern at [IIT Delhi](https://home.iitd.ac.in/) with [Prof. Tapan Kumar Gandhi](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=VyIA2PwAAAAJ&hl=en), where I worked on graph-based deep learning for anxiety-state detection and real-time seizure prediction from intracranial EEG in a rodent model. I was also a Project Intern at [MANIT Bhopal](https://www.manit.ac.in/) under [Dr. Varun Bajaj](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=zZ3vDAsAAAAJ&hl=en), where I developed an EEG–fNIRS cross-modal deep learning framework for cognitive task decoding in brain-computer interface applications.

As an IASc-INSA-NASI Summer Research Fellow at [IISc Bengaluru](https://iisc.ac.in/) under [Prof. Prasanta Kumar Ghosh](https://ee.iisc.ac.in/~prasantg/), I worked on acoustic field prediction and dysarthric speech analysis for Parkinson's disease. At [IIIT Naya Raipur](https://www.iiitnr.ac.in/) under [Dr. Anurag Singh](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=bzoViqQAAAAJ&hl=en), I developed multimodal EEG–speech deep learning frameworks for Major Depressive Disorder diagnosis.

Earlier, at [CDAC Pune](https://www.cdac.in/) with [Dr. Anil Kumar Gupta](https://www.linkedin.com/in/akgupta5592), I worked on EEG-based Parkinson's disease detection and multimodal wearable biosignal analysis. At the [University of Calcutta](https://www.caluniv.ac.in/) under [Dr. Anisha Halder Roy](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=pKsjvYMAAAAJ&hl=en), I worked on EEG–sEMG fusion for lower back pain assessment and EEG-based taste perception analysis.

Please feel free to reach out to me if you want to discuss any ideas or problems!
