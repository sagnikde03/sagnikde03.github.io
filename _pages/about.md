I completed my **B.Tech** in **Electronics and Communication Engineering** in 2025, from **Institute of Radio Physics and Electronics (IRPE)**, [University of Calcutta](https://www.caluniv.ac.in/), India. I also completed my **Minors** in **Business Administration** from [Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Patna](https://www.iitp.ac.in).

I am currently a Machine Learning Engineer at **Wekan Enterprise Solutions**, Bengaluru, benchmarking multimodal audio-text LLMs for radiology report generation and developing a PPG foundation model for arrhythmia classification from smartwatch signals.

I was a Winter Research Intern at **IIT Delhi** with [Prof. Tapan Kumar Gandhi](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=VyIA2PwAAAAJ&hl=en), working on graph-based deep learning for anxiety-state detection and real-time seizure prediction from intracranial EEG in a rodent model; and a Project Intern at **MANIT Bhopal** under [Dr. Varun Bajaj](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=zZ3vDAsAAAAJ&hl=en), developing an EEG–fNIRS cross-modal deep learning framework for cognitive task decoding in BCI applications.

As an IASc-INSA-NASI Summer Research Fellow at **IISc Bengaluru** under [Prof. Prasanta Kumar Ghosh](https://ee.iisc.ac.in/~prasantg/), I worked on acoustic field prediction and dysarthric speech analysis for Parkinson's Disease. At **IIIT Naya Raipur** under [Dr. Anurag Singh](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=bzoViqQAAAAJ&hl=en), I built multimodal EEG–speech deep learning frameworks for Major Depressive Disorder diagnosis. Earlier, at **CDAC Pune** with [Dr. Anil Kumar Gupta](https://www.linkedin.com/in/akgupta5592), I worked on EEG-based Parkinson's Disease detection and multimodal wearable biosignal analysis; and at **University of Calcutta** under [Dr. Anisha Halder Roy](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=pKsjvYMAAAAJ&hl=en), on EEG–sEMG fusion for lower back pain assessment and EEG-based taste perception analysis.
